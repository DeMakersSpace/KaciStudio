Happy Rei

CASE 006 · Happy Rei

Letting tiny reviewers make the case for comfort.

Package chosen

Social Media Management - Bespoke

Happy Rei, a kids' lifestyle brand, needed content that could show softness, usefulness, and parent trust without sounding like a catalogue. We built the central reel around the brand's most honest proof point: children choosing and rating their own favourites. The wider content set also gave the brand room to tell stories around a new store opening, the making of its pieces, and Mother's Day.

Find them on @happyrei.sg · TikTok · View reel
The Brief

Happy Rei sells pieces parents choose with care, but the strongest proof was already happening at home: children reaching for the same favourites again and again. The brief was to turn that everyday affection into content that felt real, warm, and easy to trust. A standard product showcase would have shown colour, texture, and detail, but it would not have captured the relationship between the children and the pieces they liked. The story therefore needed to work for two audiences at once. Children had to remain the natural centre of the content, while parents needed enough context to understand the comfort and usefulness behind each choice.

Our Approach

We framed the reel as a tiny review session instead of a scripted product showcase. The children led the rhythm, which kept the reactions believable and allowed the products to appear through touch, choice, and use. Close, tactile framing made softness visible without relying on a list of features. The caption then invited other parents into the conversation by asking which favourite their own little one would choose. Around that central idea, the new-store, making-of, and Mother's Day videos widened the story without changing its tone. Each format returned to the same point of view: show the care through an everyday moment, keep the language light, and let the people using the products provide the strongest reason to notice them. The products remained central without turning the children into presenters. That balance kept the series warm and useful across both product-led and occasion-led moments.

The Result

The result was a product story that felt closer to parent-to-parent proof than a catalogue pitch. The review reel made Happy Rei's comfort visible through genuine reactions, while the surrounding videos showed that the same gentle voice could carry a store moment, a behind-the-scenes process, or a seasonal story. The value sits in what the content makes visible: a repeatable, believable way for the brand to show why its pieces matter beyond how they look. It also demonstrates how our social media content and management approach can keep a lifestyle brand consistent across different campaign moments.
