Luminance Learning

CASE 007 · Luminance Learning

Turning Math anxiety into a guided conversation.

Package chosen

Social Media Management - Bespoke

Luminance Learning, a Math tuition brand, needed to speak to students and parents without making Math feel heavier than it already can. We shaped a 60-second Q&A around the questions families quietly carry: confidence, fear, foundations, and the small wins that rebuild belief. A founder-introduction video complemented that format by giving the teaching philosophy a recognisable person and voice.

Find them on @luminance_learning · View reel
The Brief

Math tuition is often introduced through grades, but the difference described by Luminance Learning is also emotional: students are seen, supported, and guided step by step. The content needed to make that care visible before a parent ever sent an enquiry. That created a delicate communication problem. The social posts still had to be clear about learning, yet leading with pressure or dramatic promises would work against the calm teaching philosophy the brand wanted families to understand. The brief was therefore to address the concerns behind tuition decisions—fear, weak foundations, and lost confidence—in language that students and parents could recognise without making those concerns feel larger.

Our Approach

We used a direct Q&A format so Mrs Khet could answer the real concerns behind Math learning in her own voice. Keeping the video to a focused 60 seconds gave each answer enough room to feel thoughtful while remaining suited to a social feed. The questions organised the story around what families wanted to know, rather than forcing the teaching philosophy into a promotional script. The founder introduction extended that trust by helping viewers connect the approach to the person delivering it. Pacing stayed clear and human, and the supporting copy positioned coaching as structured support rather than added pressure. Across both videos, the execution favoured explanation over promises and made the brand's student-first point of view easier to recognise. That gave every post a useful role in the same longer conversation about confidence.

The Result

The result was a pair of videos that gave parents and students a concise read on the teaching philosophy: calm, structured, and student-first. The Q&A shows the trust-building process behind stronger foundations and renewed belief, while the founder story makes that process feel personal. Together, they replace the pressure of a quick-fix promise with a clearer picture of structured support. That makes the brand's point of view easier to understand before a family ever begins a conversation. The case demonstrates how our social media strategy and content services can turn an education brand's philosophy into useful, human content without reducing it to a sales claim.
